What we don't charge for.

The costs that are ours to eat, not yours to pay. A coworker who bills you for its own mistakes isn't a coworker.

  • Retries we caused. If Beckett crashed, looped, or re-ran itself because of our bug, those hours are on us.
  • Failed review cycles. When our own reviewer sends work back, the rework isn't a line on your bill.
  • Our overhead. Orchestration, scheduling, the plumbing between agents, that's the cost of running the service, not a compute hour.
  • Idle presence. A seat sitting quietly in your Discord doesn't burn credits. You pay for work that ships, not for showing up.

Teams and enterprise.

Multiple seats, a shared balance, volume, invoicing terms, a private deployment: those are negotiated, not self-serve. Tell us the shape of the team and what you need, and we will write a contract to fit.

Negotiated contracts for teams
  • Volume and terms. Committed hours, shared balances across seats, and invoicing that suits your finance team.
  • Private deployment. Your own machines or cloud, your own subscription, the same compute-hour model underneath.

## When the machinery stalls — reading the distress signals

Recovery narrates itself on the run card and in update turns.

- **Stall nudges / "retrying (attempt n/m)"** — routine self-healing. Stay quiet.
- **A run PARKED after retries** — the machinery gave up; WIP is committed on the branch. Tell the
  channel where it stalled, in words. Their new direction → a steering note, and the run picks up
  from the committed work.
- **"rework cycle N/N"** then parked — implement↔review hit the cap. Read the reviewer's
  complaint, steer the run with the resolution, or relay the impasse to the human if it genuinely
  needs their call.
- **"the work is complete but could not be published"** — YOUR job; see
  `couriering-finished-work-the-dispatche.md`.
- **A run that's live but silent** — ask the worker directly (*Progress questions*) before you
  assume anything is wrong. A quiet worker is usually a working worker.
